💡 How Matching Options Work

  • Exact Match: Applies only to the exact URL specified.
  • Domain Match: Applies to all pages under that domain (e.g. example.com or *.example.com).
  • Prefix Match: Applies to all URLs starting with the prefix (e.g. https://example.com/status).
  • Wildcard Match: Supports * (matches any characters) and ? (matches single character), e.g. *example.com/*/dashboard?*.
  • Regular Expression: Full JavaScript RegExp, e.g. ^https?:\/\/(www\.)?example\.com\/(live|status)$.
  • Navigation Safety: Timers automatically disarm the moment you navigate away to an unconfigured URL.
